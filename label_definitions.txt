analysis: argues a point about the sport from a specific, checkable fact (lap times, gaps, points, tyre stints, results, a past season) where the fact is the reason for the conclusion
hot_take: a confident, general claim about a driver, team, track or the sport that offers no support, or only a fact too small to carry it
reaction: how the writer feels about something they just watched or that just happened to them, about their own experience of the moment rather than a claim about the world
banter: a post whose point is the joke (a bit, a pun, a meme, an absurd scenario) rather than a sincere claim or feeling
